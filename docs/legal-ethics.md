## Copyright & Text-and-Data Mining (EU)

| Legal basis                      | Key points for researchers                                                                                 |
| -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **EU Directive 2019/790 Art. 3** | Permits text-and-data mining (TDM) of lawfully accessible content for non-commercial scientific research.  |
| **Germany § 60d UrhG**           | Allows non-profit researchers to conduct TDM under defined conditions. |


### Practical Implications for Tidal Tales

* Collects Instagram Stories during normal website use (via Firefox plugin).
* Stores media **locally** on the file system, giving researchers full control over the collected data.
* Implements a **minimally invasive** scraping approach that mimics normal user behavior.

As a user, **you are responsible for** the following:

* Ensuring compliance with **local copyright laws and institutional ethics requirements**.
* Obtaining **informed consent** when collecting data from private profiles.
* **Pseudonymizing or anonymizing usernames** before sharing data, unless working exclusively with public figures.
* Sharing only **derived data** (e.g., annotations, metadata, embeddings), not raw media files.
